# NVIDIA Networking Portfolio

We often say the data center is the new unit of compute. ​But what really allows it to actually function as a cohesive, high-performance system is ​the network. ​Modern AI workloads, especially agentic AI and reasoning models, aren't just compute ​intensive, they're data and latency sensitive. ​These workloads don't live on a single node. ​They span thousands of GPUs, often across entire racks and rows in a data center. ​That means the network is no longer a peripheral component, it's the foundation. ​To meet these needs, NVIDIA provides complete end-to-end solutions supporting InfiniBand ​and Ethernet networking technologies. 
​InfiniBand and Spectrum X are our two leading platforms that form the high-speed East-West ​fabric that connects compute nodes inside the data center. ​These technologies are critical for scaling out training and inference across multiple ​GPUs and nodes. ​This is what we call the East-West or compute network. ​While InfiniBand and Spectrum X are designed for scale-out, NVLink is how we scale up. ​NVLink is the high-bandwidth interconnect that allows multiple GPUs within a server ​or across tightly coupled servers to act like a single large memory and compute pool. ​This is essential for modern parallelism, where a single AI model spans multiple GPUs ​with minimal overhead. ​The Bluefield DPUs are positioned for securing and accelerating north-south traffic, or what ​comes in and out of the data center, including user access, control planes, and storage fabrics. 
​By offloading networking, security, and storage functions from the CPU to the DPU, Bluefield ​frees up host resources for actual computation while improving performance and isolation. ​Let's explore the NVIDIA Spectrum X switches specifically designed for Ethernet use within ​AI data centers. ​The NVIDIA Spectrum X networking platform is the first Ethernet platform designed specifically ​to improve the performance and efficiency of Ethernet-based AI clouds. ​It delivers the nearly perfect bandwidth and extremely low latency needed for AI workloads. ​The key to this performance boost are the RoCE extensions that NVIDIA has introduced. ​Going beyond standard RoCE, these extensions include RoCE Adaptive Routing for dynamic ​load balancing and RoCE Congestion Control for performance isolation. ​We'll go over these RoCE extensions later in this unit. 
​These innovative extensions are unique to NVIDIA and only work with the combination ​of the NVIDIA Spectrum 4 Ethernet switch and the NVIDIA Bluefield 3 data processing units. ​The combination of the Spectrum 4 Ethernet switch and NVIDIA Bluefield 3 DPUs installed ​on compute servers improves the standard Ethernet protocol by minimizing congestion and latency ​while improving bandwidth. ​The Spectrum X platform also undergoes rigorous tuning and validation across the entire NVIDIA ​hardware and software stack, ensuring an unparalleled Ethernet solution for AI clouds. ​With that said, Spectrum X uses standards-based Ethernet and is fully interoperable with any ​device that communicates with Ethernet. ​NVIDIA's RoCE Adaptive Routing is a fine-grained load balancing technology that dynamically ​reroutes RDMA data to avoid congestion and provide optimal load balancing. ​It improves network utilization by selecting forwarding paths dynamically based on the ​state of the switch, such as queue occupancy and port utilization. ​NVIDIA's RoCE Congestion Control is a mechanism used to reduce packet drops in lossy networks ​or congestion spreading in lossless networks. 
​It limits the injection rate of flows at the ports causing congestion, thereby reducing ​switch buffer occupancy, decreasing latency, and improving burst tolerance. ​NVIDIA's RoCE Adaptive Routing and Congestion Control Ethernet enhancements require Spectrum ​4 switch and Bluefield 3 DPU to work in unison. ​Now let's turn to the NVIDIA Quantum X800 platform. ​The NVIDIA Quantum X800 platform is the next generation of NVIDIA Quantum InfiniBand purpose-built ​for trillion-parameter scale AI models and comprised of the NVIDIA Quantum X800 InfiniBand ​switch, NVIDIA ConnectX 8 SuperNIC, and NVIDIA LinkX cables and transceivers. ​It is the highest-performance end-to-end 800 Gbps networking designed for massive-scale ​AI. ​The NVIDIA Quantum X800 InfiniBand switch provides 144 ports of 800 Gbps connectivity ​per port. ​It includes hardware-based in-network computing with SHARPv4, adaptive routing, telemetry-based ​congestion control, performance isolation capabilities, and a dedicated port supporting ​the Unified Fabric Manager, or UFM. 
​This platform also integrates the NVIDIA ConnectX 8 SuperNIC. ​The NVIDIA ConnectX 8 SuperNIC is purpose-built to accelerate hyperscale AI workloads, delivering ​up to 800 Gbps of high-speed network connectivity over both InfiniBand and Ethernet. ​Its PCI Express Gen6 interface supports up to 48 lanes, offering flexible deployment ​options for applications such as PCIe switching within NVIDIA GPU systems. ​This new platform delivers the highest performance for dedicated AI infrastructure, enabling ​a new frontier of AI innovation. ​NVIDIA is revolutionizing networking for the era of agentic AI by introducing co-packaged ​optics in the NVIDIA Quantum X800 platform. ​NVIDIA's co-packaged optics switches with integrated silicon photonics are the world's ​most advanced networking solution for the era of agentic AI. ​Replacing pluggable transceivers with silicon photonics on the same package as the ASIC, ​NVIDIA co-packaged optics innovations provide 3.5 times better power efficiency, 10 times ​higher network resiliency, and 1.3 times faster time to deploy compared to traditional networks. 
​The Quantum 3450LD switch is our next-generation 115Tbps InfiniBand photonics platform with ​144 ports of 800Gbps InfiniBand. ​This groundbreaking switch leverages a liquid-cooled design to efficiently cool the onboard silicon ​photonics. ​The NVIDIA Quantum X Photonics InfiniBand switch supports new network innovations to ​deliver unprecedented scale, connecting over 10,000 GPUs in a non-blocking, two-level fat-tree ​topology at 800Gbps for AI factory scale-out with InfiniBand. ​NVIDIA is also enabling co-packaged optics for the next generation of Ethernet switches ​with Spectrum X. ​This includes the Spectrum SN6810 switch featuring 128 ports of 800Gbps Ethernet and the Spectrum ​SN6800 switch featuring 512 ports of 800Gbps Ethernet. ​NVIDIA Photonic innovations are transforming AI infrastructure, paving the way for agentic ​AI, AI factories, and the next generation of accelerated computing.
